End-to-End Workflow

How the Redistribution Network Functions

A closed-loop, auditable supply chain connecting surplus food producers with grassroots hunger relief workers.

1. Listing Published

Donors (restaurants, caterers, hotels) declare surplus quantity, preparation time, storage conditions, and pickup cutoff deadline.

2. Triage & Notification

Our Urgency Engine calculates perishability risk scores and alerts verified NGOs and volunteers operating within the donor's service radius.

3. Claim & Pickup

An authorized NGO or volunteer accepts the donation. They arrive on-site, inspect safety seals, and confirm pickup electronically.

4. Safe Distribution

Meals are transported in insulated bags to community shelters or elderly homes. Delivery is logged with an immutable audit trail.

Impact Across the Ecosystem

Designed to benefit every participant in the civic food redistribution network.

For Donors
  • Eliminate commercial kitchen disposal costs and reduce municipal dumpster burden.
  • Transparent tracking: Know exactly when and which verified NGO picks up your food.
  • Fulfill Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and zero-waste sustainability goals.
For NGOs & Volunteers
  • Real-time alerts on urgent, fresh meals ready for rapid dispatch.
  • No double-claiming conflicts thanks to transactional database reservation locks.
  • Clear preparation logs, allergen information, and storage conditions before dispatch.
For Communities & India
  • Mitigate methane emissions generated by organic landfill decomposition.
  • Provide dignified, warm nutrition to vulnerable urban populations.
  • Support UN Sustainable Development Goal 12.3 (Halving global food waste by 2030).
Rigorous Food Safety Principles

Safety & Hygiene Are Non-Negotiable

Food safety regulations mandate that donated meals remain strictly within safe thermal zones. Cooked meals must be collected and served before the danger zone window induces bacterial growth.

Preparation timestamps recorded for all batches
Mandatory thermal storage declarations
Automated expiry locks when cutoff passes
One-click safety flag with admin escalation
Report a Concern Anytime

Any collector can flag questionable odors, unsealed packaging, or improper storage temperatures directly in the app.
